My WHY (Brief statement on my teaching philosophy):

I believe every child deserves to feel seen, valued, safe, and capable of success. As a kaiako, my role is not only to teach academic skills but also to nurture confidence, curiosity, kindness, and a love for learning. I strive to create a classroom where tamariki feel a strong sense of belonging, where relationships come first, and where mistakes are viewed as part of growth.

Guided by our WHARE values, I aim to provide engaging, inclusive, and culturally responsive learning experiences that honour each child’s identity, voice, and strengths. I believe learning happens best when students feel connected to their peers, whānau, and community. Through collaboration, encouragement, and high expectations, I want my learners to become resilient, respectful, and lifelong learners who believe in themselves and positively contribute to the world around them.

My professional inquiry focus:

How can I create a culturally responsive, engaging, and supportive learning environment that strengthens student wellbeing, literacy, numeracy, and learner confidence while ensuring all tamariki feel a sense of belonging and success in the classroom?

My inquiry will focus on strengthening effective teaching practices through relationship-based learning, explicit teaching, student voice, and meaningful learning experiences aligned with our WHARE values and local curriculum. I aim to reflect on how my teaching supports learner progress, engagement, inclusion, and positive behaviour, particularly for students requiring additional support or acceleration.

Through ongoing reflection, collaboration with colleagues, whānau partnerships, assessment data, and professional learning, I will continue to grow my practice to better meet the diverse needs of all learners in my classroom.
